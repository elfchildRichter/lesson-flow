1. 服務簡介與授權範疇

課伴 LessonFlow 以下簡稱本服務，由 ArchCet 開發營運，專為教育工作者提供 AI 輔助之教材解析、課程簡報大綱、逐頁講稿、隨堂講義與評量試卷生成服務，致力於提供開放透明且專業可靠之備課工具。

2. 使用者責任與教材規範

使用者上傳之 PDF 教材及產出之教學文件，須確保具備合法使用權利或符合教學合理使用原則。嚴禁利用本服務產出任何違法、侵權或具危害性之內容。使用者對其帳號所生成之簡報、講義與試卷等教學資產，享有完整之教學應用與衍生改作權利。

3. AI 生成免責與專業審查原則

本服務運用先進多模態與語言模型協助教學設計。AI 產出之簡報大綱、講稿、LaTeX 數學公式與評量試題僅供教學輔助參考，教師應於課堂施教或正式評量前進行專業審定與校對。

本服務採每日備課點數制，依使用者等級每日自動配發額度。每日點數於 UTC 00:00 自動刷新，未用完之每日額度不予遞延至次日。各項核心備課功能之點數扣除標準如下：

LessonFlow Terms of Service

Last Revised: September 2026 · ArchCet

1. Service Introduction & Authorization

LessonFlow (referred to as "the Service"), developed and operated by ArchCet, provides educators with AI-assisted textbook parsing, slide outlines, per-slide speaker notes, A4 handouts, and quiz generation tools, committed to open, transparent, and professional teaching preparation.

2. User Responsibilities & Material Guidelines

Users uploading PDF textbooks and generating teaching assets must ensure they hold lawful authorization or comply with fair use principles in education. Utilizing the Service to generate unlawful, infringing, or harmful content is strictly prohibited. Users retain full teaching rights and derivative creation rights over all materials generated through their accounts.

3. AI Disclaimer & Professional Review

The Service leverages advanced multimodal and language models to assist instructional design. AI-generated outlines, speaker notes, LaTeX formulas, and quizzes serve as teaching references only. Educators should conduct professional verification and proofreading before classroom delivery or formal assessment.

4. Lesson Preparation Credits & Deduction Rules

The Service operates on a daily preparation credit quota, automatically refreshed daily at 00:00 UTC according to user tiers. Unused daily credits do not carry over to the next day. Standard credit deduction rates are as follows:

Feature Credits Description
Generate Slide Deck 50 pts Includes slide outline, bullet points, visual suggestions, and full speaker notes
Generate A4 Handout 30 pts Includes reading preview, key concepts summary, and discussion questions
Generate Unit Quiz 20 pts Includes 5 questions, distractor analysis, and detailed explanations
Multimodal Visual & Formula Analysis 30 pts Visual model for high-resolution diagrams, tables, and scientific formulas
Standard Text Embedding Analysis 5 pts Text indexing and chapter semantic vector retrieval
AI Fine-Tuning & Polish 3 pts Polish slide speaker notes or regenerate single quiz items
Teaching Assistant Q&A Chat 1 pt Sourced question-answering with page citations and concept breakdown

5. Service Modifications & Maintenance

ArchCet continuously optimizes system stability and generation quality. ArchCet reserves the right to suspend or adjust services during system maintenance, network anomalies, or third-party provider disruptions.
