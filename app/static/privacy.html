課伴 LessonFlow 隱私權政策

最後修訂日期：2026 年 9 月 · ArchCet

1. 資料收集項目

當您註冊或使用本服務時，我們僅收集維持系統運作所需之必要資訊，包括使用者帳號、經加鹽雜湊加密之密碼、登入時間戳記與每日備課點數使用記錄。

2. 教材文件私隱與資料保護

您上傳之 PDF 教材及教學資源，僅於伺服器記憶體中進行當次會話之向量索引與教學解析處理。我們承諾絕不會將您的私有教材與教案內容用於任何公開大語言模型之訓練，亦絕不對外共享或轉售任何教學資產。

3. 資料傳輸與儲存安全機制

全站網路傳輸均採用標準 HTTPS 加密協議；帳號密碼經由加鹽雜湊技術妥善保護；會話記憶體索引與臨時檔案均具備嚴格之安全生命週期銷毀機制。

4. 第三方 AI 服務串接合規

本服務串接企業級 AI 服務介面時，均遵循嚴格之商務隱私與資料不留存協定，全力保障教學資產之隱私安全。

5. 使用者個人資料權利

使用者可隨時查閱、更新或請求刪除個人帳號與相關使用記錄。

6. 聯絡窗口

若您對個人資料處理或隱私權政策有任何疑問，歡迎寄信至 contact@archcet.com 與我們聯繫。

LessonFlow Privacy Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 · ArchCet

1. Information We Collect

When you register or use the Service, we only collect information necessary to maintain system operations, including your username, salted and hashed password, login timestamps, and daily credit usage records.

2. Teaching Materials Privacy & Data Protection

Your uploaded PDF textbooks and educational materials are processed in server memory solely for vector indexing and session-level instructional analysis. We will never use your private teaching materials or lesson plans to train any public large language models, nor will we share or sell your teaching assets to any third party.

3. Data Transmission & Security Mechanisms

All network transmissions across the platform utilize standard HTTPS encryption protocols. Account credentials are protected using salted hashing algorithms. In-memory session indices and temporary files adhere to strict lifecycle destruction mechanisms.

4. Third-Party AI Service Compliance

When integrating enterprise-grade AI model APIs, the Service complies with strict commercial privacy and zero data retention agreements to ensure teaching asset confidentiality.

5. User Data Rights

Users may inspect, update, or request deletion of their personal accounts and associated usage records at any time.

6. Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding personal data processing or this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@archcet.com.
